Email: shuangqing.xu@outlook.com
I am currently a Ph.D. student at the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, and the School of Computer Science and Technology, Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen. My research focuses on the intersection of cryptography, security, and machine learning, particularly in areas of privacy-preserving computation and federated learning. I have been honored to receive the Distinguished Paper Award from ACM CCS 2025.
